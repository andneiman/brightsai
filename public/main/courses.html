What you get back

Here is the same Grade 6 Math course after personalization, 72 hours after you enrolled. The unit sequence you saw in the catalog is unchanged and the learning objective is unchanged — what moved is the context, the format, and the length of each practice set.

  • The objective stays fixed: Find a percentage of a quantity and explain the reasoning.
  • The context changes: Examples are rewritten around what the student already cares about.
  • The format follows the profile: A visual learner gets charts and pictures, not walls of text.
  • The path adapts: Module order, pacing, and practice length follow the diagnostic.

What happens after you pick one

Three steps, and only the first one asks anything of you.

Step 1

You choose a course

Pick a subject and grade from the catalog above, fill in a short parent questionnaire, and let your child take the diagnostic. You tell us the goals, the interests, and how much time there is in a week.

Step 2

We build it around your child

Our teachers select and sequence the modules, set the pacing, and rewrite examples, practice, projects, and review to fit the profile you described. It takes 72 hours, and then the finished course is in your account.

Step 3

Learn at your own pace

Your child can pause, go back over a topic, or move ahead. You watch it happen in the parent dashboard, where you review completion, assessment results, strengths, and next steps.

A personalized curriculum you can document clearly

Each course has a defined subject, grade or level, learning objectives, scope and sequence, included materials, access period, assessment plan, and price. This helps families understand the purchase and prepare the documentation required by their ESA program.

  • What families purchase: A complete student-specific curriculum package for a defined subject, grade or level, and access period.
  • What it is not: Personalization adapts an established academic course; it does not turn the product into an open-ended teaching or tutoring service.
